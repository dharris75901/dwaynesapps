🪚 Plywood Box Cut Calculator

Enter your box sizes and the sides you need. It builds the cut list and packs it onto the fewest sheets.

Plywood & Saw

Fractions are fine: 23 1/2, 23-1/2 or 23.5. All sizes in inches.

Boxes

Construction: front & back run the full length, the two ends fit between them, and the top & bottom lay over the walls at full length × width.

Shelves & Extra Panels

Flat pieces cut to an exact size: shelves, dividers, backs. Enter in inches (72 = 6 ft).

Put this app on your Home Screen

Takes about 30 seconds. Then it opens with one tap, like a regular app.

Using Safari (recommended)

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an up arrow). On newer iPhones, tap the ••• button by the address bar first, then Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. Don't see it? Tap View More or Edit Actions.
  4. If you see Open as Web App, leave it on.
  5. Tap Add in the top-right corner.

Using Chrome or Edge

  1. Tap the Share button next to the address bar.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
Can't find it? Copy the web address, open it in Safari, and follow the Safari steps.

Good to know

  • Move it: press and hold the icon, then drag.
  • Remove it: press and hold, then tap Remove App. Only the shortcut is removed.
  • Updates: nothing to install; you always get the latest version.
