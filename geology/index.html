Mohs hardness scale

Each mineral scratches the ones above it.

Field test: fingernail ≈ 2.5 · copper penny ≈ 3.5 · steel knife or nail ≈ 5.5 · glass ≈ 5.5 · steel file ≈ 6.5. If a knife won't scratch it but it scratches glass, it's harder than 5.5, like quartz.

Geologic time scale

As used by geologists today; "mya" = million years ago.
